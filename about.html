OUR HISTORY

It started with
a mobile DJ.

Founder Jason Bank began as a mobile DJ for local parties before developing Jammin’ Express Entertainment into a broader event production business. The company has operated in South Florida since 1993.

Today, the team includes DJs, MCs and party motivators, offering event planning support and a range of entertainment and production services.

01 / SERVICES

Jason Bank, Founder

Jason built Jammin’ Express around entertainment and service. He was named President of the American Disc Jockey Association of South Florida.

02 / SERVICES

An Experienced Team

DJs, MCs and party motivators work together on the music, guest interaction and structure of the event. The company says its entertainers receive training and participate in monthly team meetings.

03 / SERVICES

Serving South Florida

Jammin’ Express is based in Weston and works throughout the Tri-County area, including communities in Broward, Palm Beach and Miami-Dade counties.
