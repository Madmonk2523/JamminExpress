CEREMONY TO RECEPTION

Every part of the day
has its own soundtrack.

The ceremony, cocktail hour, introductions, first dance and reception each have a different pace. Thoughtful music selection and hosting help connect those parts of the day.

Jammin’ Express offers DJ and MC services alongside specialty music, lighting, officiant and video options.

01 / SETTING THE TONE

Ceremony & Cocktail Hour

Discuss ceremony sound, processional music and cocktail-hour selections with the team. The aim is a seamless transition into the reception.

02 / MUSIC & HOSTING

The Reception

A DJ and MC can coordinate introductions, announcements, key moments and dance-floor music according to your plans and your guests.

03 / PERSONAL DETAILS

Specialty Entertainment

Options listed by Jammin’ Express include live vocalists, violin, guitar, mandolin, steel drums and other custom additions.
