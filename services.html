THE ENTERTAINMENT TEAM

Coordinated from
start to finish.

Our team brings together music, professional hosting, dance-floor entertainment and event enhancements. Each part can be planned according to your venue, guest list and celebration.

Explore the services below. For customized requests, we can discuss options specific to your event.

01 / SERVICES

Professional DJs & MCs

DJs create the musical flow of the occasion while MCs handle announcements, introductions and guest engagement. Music can be discussed in advance, including preferences and important moments.

02 / SERVICES

Party Motivators

Dancers and party motivators lead group activities, get guests involved and help maintain momentum across the event. They can be particularly useful at celebrations with guests of different ages.

03 / SERVICES

Interactive Entertainment

Options include Face-Off, Group Trivia and other group games. These can be incorporated as part of the event schedule.

04 / SERVICES

Lighting & Production

Uplighting, dance lighting, screens, cold sparks, special effects and other enhancements are listed among the company’s offerings. Suitability depends on the event and venue.
