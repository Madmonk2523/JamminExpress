WHAT TYPES OF EVENTS DO YOU DO?+

Bar and Bat Mitzvahs, weddings, Sweet 16s, quinceañeras, corporate events, school celebrations, holiday parties and more. If there's something to celebrate, let's talk.

CAN WE CUSTOMIZE THE ENTERTAINMENT?+

Absolutely. We plan with your vision in mind, from DJs and MCs to party motivators, games, lighting, photo booths, videography and special effects.

DO YOU OFFER GAME SHOWS?+

Yes! Our interactive options include Face-Off and Group Trivia, with options for adding fun competition to your celebration.

WHERE DO YOU WORK?+

We are based in Weston and serve South Florida, including Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach county communities.
