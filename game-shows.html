01 / BUZZERS & PODIUMS

Face-Off

A two- or four-person contest format featuring podiums and a buzzer system. Players compete to respond first, while other guests can watch and cheer.

02 / TEAM PARTICIPATION

Group Trivia

Bring guests together around a shared trivia challenge, using questions suited to the audience and tone of the event.
