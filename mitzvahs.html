THE OCCASION

Their milestone.
A celebration for everyone.

A Bar or Bat Mitzvah is an important occasion for your family. The entertainment needs to balance meaningful traditions with a celebration that works for both younger and older guests.

Our team works with you on the flow of the event, music preferences, introductions, and ways to get everyone involved.

01 / MUSIC & HOSTING

DJs & MCs

Our DJs shape the music around the moments of the event. MCs lead introductions, coordinate with the entertainment team and keep the celebration moving.

02 / ON THE DANCE FLOOR

Party Motivators

Professional dancers and party motivators help bring guests of different ages into the celebration. They can lead group dances, games, and interactive moments.

03 / GROUP PARTICIPATION

Interactive Games

Options such as Face-Off and group trivia can be included to give guests something to enjoy beyond the dance floor.
